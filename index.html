About Jay

Telling stories in color, wherever the road leads.

Based between Colombia, Spain, and Canada, I photograph the people and moments that make each culture feel alive — traditional dress, festival color, quiet portraits, and everything in between.

What started as a way to document travel has grown into a full creative practice: shoots, reels, and campaigns built around real emotion instead of a stiff pose.
